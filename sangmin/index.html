Hi, I’m Sangmin, a moss farmer in Goyang, just northwest of Seoul. I find possibilities in nature.

I turn moss into products, from terrariums to commemorative plaques. Making and selling them taught me to connect materials with real needs.

With Urban Jungle, I design and build gardens and school forests. We make places where people can spend time with nature.

Questions from the field also become software. Garden Atlas is a prototype for exploring landscape designs and sharing expert feedback.

I make board games, tree cards, and published books. They give people a playful way to get closer to nature.

I also plan and run cultural festivals. Gardens become places to meet, share stories, and learn from each other.

What could we make together? Your local materials, knowledge, or community could meet my experience in one small experiment.

Send me a photo or a question. Or visit Hacker Farm in Goyang, and let’s have a cup of tea.

I grow moss and turn it into frames, terrariums and commemorative plaques. A little about me

I like making things—and making them with people.

Connection Designer
I connect nature, people and the systems between them.

I'm a moss farmer. I grow moss at our farm in Goyang and, with Urban Jungle, turn it into products: moss frames, terrariums, plaques. We also design and build gardens, school forests and show gardens.

I’ve developed, made and sold products, run garden projects, and organised places for people to gather. I’d love to share the practical skills and the lessons learned along the way with people in other countries.

I also connect people through garden, greening and forest associations. These days I’m learning tree climbing. I’d love to visit each other’s forests and explore new uses for the materials around us.
