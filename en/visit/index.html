What happens on the first day?

You do not need technical experience to begin. We are preparing a day of looking together, trying something by hand and leaving one record for the next person.

Proposed first-day schedule. Dates and facilitators are not confirmed. No reservations or payments are open.

  1. 00–20 min

    Meet and find your bearings

    Review the route and tool precautions, then share what you want to try and what support you need.

  2. 20–50 min

    Observe the same place

    Start with a photo and a short observation. A paper option is planned for people who prefer not to use a phone.

  3. 50–65 min

    Take a break

    Rest and choose the next activity. Seating and rest areas will be confirmed after a site check.

  4. 65–140 min

    Try something in a small team

    Choose farm observation, tool records or paper and light. Electrical and cutting tools will require a site safety check before use.

  5. 140–180 min

    Leave a record and a question

    Finish an observation sheet, tool condition card or light sketch. Share what you would change, regardless of polish.

Take home one personal record. Leave a question someone else can build on.

Six meetings across twelve weeks

  1. Observe and choose a question
  2. Design a way to record
  3. Make a small prototype
  4. Try again and repair
  5. Exchange with another team
  6. Share results and failures
