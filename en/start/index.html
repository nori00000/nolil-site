The plan includes six sessions, basic shared tools and consumables, and documentation guidance. Personal equipment, specialist materials, meals and transport are outside the plan. Start dates, facilitators, detailed support and refund terms will be published before enrolment. The model does not rely on property rental revenue.

Questions before you start.

Do I need technical skills?

You can start by observing, explaining or sketching without specialist equipment. Each on-site activity will specify the experience needed and the support available.

Can I visit or sign up?

We are preparing our first work session. Please wait until the date, location and costs are announced before arranging to take part. Choosing an activity here does not make a booking or submit an application.

Do I have to share my work publicly?

Sharing your work, photos or notes, and choosing to contribute, are voluntary. Attribution and reuse requirements for source material still apply.

How does the work continue?

For example: find a question in your observation notes → compare it with other records → adjust the question or tools → leave an explanation for the next step. This is a proposed way of working, not a confirmed programme or a record of member results.

Can I take part in English?

These pages are available in English. Language support for on-site activities has not yet been confirmed. We will state the available languages when activity details are published.
