The first-season proposal

Proposal awaiting a field pilot. Membership recruitment, reservations and payments have not opened.

Group size
12 people
Season length
12 weeks
Fee per person
KRW 360,000
Scheduled sessions
6 × 3 hours

Proposed inclusions

Scheduled sessions, basic shared tools and consumables, and documentation guidance.

Outside the plan

Personal equipment, specialist materials, meals and transport. Membership covers scheduled sessions, not unrestricted access to the premises.

To confirm before recruitment

Dates and facilitators; exact tools and consumables; insurance and safety support; refunds; parking, toilets, seating, accessible routes, wet-weather arrangements and on-site languages.

Shared-farm membership fees support joint activities. The model does not rely on property rental income.
