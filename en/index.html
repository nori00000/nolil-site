01 / FIRST DAY

What happens on the first day?

You do not need technical experience to begin. We are preparing a day of looking together, trying something by hand and leaving one record for the next person.

Take home one personal record. Leave a question someone else can build on. →

How we work

We start with a question.
Then take it further.

We study openly shared farming and making projects, then adapt them to the tools available today and the conditions on our farm. Technical function and artistic enquiry have equal room here.
