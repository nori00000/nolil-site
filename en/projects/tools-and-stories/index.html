02

Small steps
to get started.

For now, these are things you can try at home. We will share on-site dates and support once they are confirmed.

01

Find a point of confusion

Recall one recent moment when you had trouble finding or using an object. Write down what happened and what explanation would have helped.

Start with your own experience; no repair skills are needed.
Start here ↗
02

Explain it to a first-time user

Choose an object at home. Write its name, where it belongs and any conditions for using it.

Start with short sentences; no experience making QR codes is needed.
Start here ↗
03

Record its condition before repair

Describe the appearance and symptoms of an object you would like to repair. You do not need to take it apart or test it.

Start by recording its condition. Practical repairs come later, after experience and the person responsible have been confirmed.
Start here ↗

What we need to check next.
