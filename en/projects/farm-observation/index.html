02

Small steps
to get started.

For now, these are things you can try at home. We will share on-site dates and support once they are confirmed.

01

Record the light in one place

Choose a place nearby. Write three sentences about the light and colours you see right now.

Start with photos and notes; no experience with measuring equipment is needed.
Start here ↗
02

Choose what to record

Choose what you would like to record. Write a line for the date, time, units and a symbol for values you do not know.

Start by reading rows and columns; coding can come later if you choose.
Start here ↗
03

Capture a day in colour and words

Think of the light on a day you remember. Express it in three colours or a short piece of writing.

Choose your own form; no experience with editing software is needed.
Start here ↗
