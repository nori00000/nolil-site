1. Hacker Farm

Observe, repair and create at a shared farm in Goyang, South Korea.

2. A shared place.

A farm where questions and projects continue beyond a single visit.

  • Record a change and compare it on your next visit.
  • Repair a tool and leave instructions for the next person.
  • Explore technology and art on their own terms.

3. Look closer.

Put photographs and impressions alongside readings of light, temperature and soil.

  • Source principle: TechRice links places, devices, sensors and readings
  • Adaptation: add timestamps, units, missing data and human observations
  • Your starting point: three sentences about light and colour in one place

4. Keep a note.

Put photographs and impressions alongside readings of light, temperature and soil.

  • Source principle: TechRice links places, devices, sensors and readings
  • Adaptation: add timestamps, units, missing data and human observations
  • Your starting point: three sentences about light and colour in one place

5. Use it again.

Explain where a tool is, its condition and how it can be used again.

  • Source principle: QR Inventory connects an item ID with its status
  • Adaptation: add photos, checks, repair history and short instructions
  • Your starting point: a name, location and condition card for one object

6. Make light.

Explore structure, material and energy to give your question a physical form.

  • Source idea: Solar Paper Lantern brings light, paper and energy together
  • Adaptation: begin with paper models, then assess current components and safety
  • Your starting point: sketch a paper structure and mark a path for light

7. Come curious.

Choose an interest, try a small action and leave yourself a next question.

  • 01 Discover — choose a question about observation, tools or light
  • 02 Prepare — start on your own with a photo, note or sketch
  • 03 Work together — join a small team in the planned first season
  • 04 Continue — use what you record to begin the next experiment

8. 12 weeks

Six three-hour sessions, every other week. Designed around three teams of four.

  • Sessions 1–2 · explore the setting and choose questions and roles
  • Sessions 3–4 · build small prototypes, compare records and revise
  • Sessions 5–6 · test again and document the next steps

9. 12 people

Six three-hour sessions, every other week. Designed around three teams of four.

  • Sessions 1–2 · explore the setting and choose questions and roles
  • Sessions 3–4 · build small prototypes, compare records and revise
  • Sessions 5–6 · test again and document the next steps

10. ₩360,000

First-season capacity: 12 people · KRW 360,000 per person for 12 weeks

  • Planned inclusions: six sessions, basic shared tools and consumables, documentation guidance
  • Outside the plan: personal equipment, specialist materials, meals and transport
  • Membership covers scheduled sessions, not unrestricted access to the premises.
  • Start dates, facilitators, detailed support and refund terms will be published before enrolment.

11. Share. Care.

Membership fees support preparation and the running of shared activities.

  • Our operating model does not rely on property rental revenue.
  • Different contributions through technology, art and observation are valued.
  • You choose whether to share your work, photos and records.
  • We distinguish sources from adaptations, and plans from actual results.

12. One question.

Enrolment is being prepared. Read the material and choose a personal starting point.

  • Explore the three projects and the preparation suggested for each role.
  • Write your question and save a personal note.
  • Twelve is the planned capacity. No members have been recruited yet.
  • Reading this material or saving a note does not submit an application or booking.
