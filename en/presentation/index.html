OUR DIFFERENCE

The farm is where
the work begins.

Weather, materials, signs of use and human perception raise new questions each time.

  1. Place — return to the same spot and notice what changes
  2. Continuity — pair repair with useful documentation
  3. Expression — value artistic inquiry in its own right

01 / OBSERVE

Read the farm’s
less visible changes.

Put photographs and impressions alongside readings of light, temperature and soil.

  1. Source principle: TechRice links places, devices, sensors and readings
  2. Adaptation: add timestamps, units, missing data and human observations
  3. Your starting point: three sentences about light and colour in one place

02 / REPAIR

Think of the next
person using a tool.

Explain where a tool is, its condition and how it can be used again.

  1. Source principle: QR Inventory connects an item ID with its status
  2. Adaptation: add photos, checks, repair history and short instructions
  3. Your starting point: a name, location and condition card for one object

Express light through

HACKER FARM STUDIO

YOUR JOURNEY

Begin with curiosity,
not expertise.

Choose an interest, try a small action and leave yourself a next question.

  1. 01 Discover — choose a question about observation, tools or light
  2. 02 Prepare — start on your own with a photo, note or sketch
  3. 03 Work together — join a small team in the planned first season
  4. 04 Continue — use what you record to begin the next experiment

HACKER FARM STUDIO

FIRST SEASON / PLAN

Twelve weeks of
small experiments.

Six three-hour sessions, every other week. Designed around three teams of four.

  1. Sessions 1–2 · explore the setting and choose questions and roles
  2. Sessions 3–4 · build small prototypes, compare records and revise
  3. Sessions 5–6 · test again and document the next steps

HACKER FARM STUDIO

MEMBERSHIP / PLAN

A clear group size
and membership fee.

First-season capacity: 12 people · KRW 360,000 per person for 12 weeks

  1. Planned inclusions: six sessions, basic shared tools and consumables, documentation guidance
  2. Outside the plan: personal equipment, specialist materials, meals and transport
  3. Membership covers scheduled sessions, not unrestricted access to the premises.
  4. Start dates, facilitators, detailed support and refund terms will be published before enrolment.

HACKER FARM STUDIO

OUR PRINCIPLES

A shared farm.
Shared commitments.

Membership fees support preparation and the running of shared activities.

  1. Our operating model does not rely on property rental revenue.
  2. Different contributions through technology, art and observation are valued.
  3. You choose whether to share your work, photos and records.
  4. We distinguish sources from adaptations, and plans from actual results.
