A first look at Hacker Farm

Hacker Farm is a shared farm project in Goyang, South Korea, exploring technology and art through observation, repair and creative work. Its first membership season is in preparation.

As of 2026-10-10 · Preparing the first season · No applications, bookings or payments

What is Hacker Farm?

Hacker Farm is a shared farm project in Goyang, South Korea. STUDIO is a secondary descriptor. We are preparing activities to observe changes on the farm, repair tools and create with light and materials, leaving records others can build on.

What can a beginner do?

The three planned areas are farm observation, tools and stories, and farm light. Explore roles that start with observation notes, tool descriptions or sketches without specialist equipment. Choosing a role and saving a personal note on this site are preparation, not an application.

What are the proposed fee and group size?

The first-season plan is 12 people for 12 weeks at KRW 360,000 per person: six three-hour sessions every other week, in three teams of four. The plan includes regular sessions, basic shared tools and consumables, and recording guidance. Personal equipment, special materials, meals and travel are separate. Dates, facilitators, detailed support and refund terms will be published before enrollment.

Can I visit or join now?

No members have been recruited. Applications, bookings and payments are not open. We will publish the location, schedule and activity terms before enrollment. The operating model is shared farm membership fees, without property rental revenue.

How are the referenced projects related to Hacker Farm?

The 17 research groups in our library are references. Original sources and our adaptation plans are labelled separately; a reference does not imply affiliation or official branch status. Not all sources have been translated, implemented or field-tested.

Which languages are available?

The website is available in Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish and French. Language support for on-site activities has not yet been confirmed and will be stated with enrollment terms.
