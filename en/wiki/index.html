Name and identity

Hacker Farm is a shared farm exploring questions through technology and art. HACKER FARM is the main name; STUDIO is a small secondary descriptor. We observe, repair and create, leaving records that others can build on.

Operating principles and current status

We are designing a membership fee model for a shared farm, without property rental revenue. No members have been recruited. The first-season plan is for 12 people at KRW 360,000 per person for 12 weeks, with six three-hour sessions every other week. Start dates, facilitators, detailed support and refunds are being prepared. Applications, bookings and payments are not open. Sharing personal work, photographs or records is voluntary. Technology and art are respected as independent areas of inquiry.

How we update earlier work

We do not discard a project simply because it is old. We first document its question, structure and working principles, then adapt it to available components, software and our setting. Our sequence is source review → principles to retain → adaptation design → small prototype → field trial → record failures and reproduction steps. The work below is a plan, not evidence of completed builds or validated performance.

Three adaptation plans

PRJ01 / Design · Before field testing

A day on the farm, seen differently ↗

Start with a question from the field, then connect observation with technology.

  1. Observation records that include time, units and errors
  2. Korean-language exercises that others can run and reproduce
  3. Graphs and creative work that bring measurements and sensory impressions together

Next check: Locate the original logs, check their format and build an example that others can reproduce.

PRJ02 / Design · Before field testing

Tools the next person can use ↗

Use a stable identifier to connect a physical object with its location and condition.

  1. Tool records that are easy to read on a phone
  2. A history of conditions before and after repair, with the next check recorded
  3. Labels that help the next person find and use a tool

Next check: Choose an actual tool, check its condition and design a record for it.

PRJ03 / Design · Before field testing

Making light on the farm ↗

Bring light, paper and energy together as a form of expression.

  1. Available materials and structures that can be adapted
  2. Instructions that make assembly and repair possible
  3. Working notes that compare the intended effect of light with the experience of it

Next check: Set out the creative intention and proposed materials, then define conditions for testing a small model.

Personal notes and selection links

Questions entered on the Take part page remain in page memory and are cleared on reload. Your browser may restore them when you go back. You can download a personal file or copy the text. Shared selection links include only the project and role, never your question. This website is not a membership application or payment form. It uses no separate analytics or advertising scripts. Technical request processing by the web host is separate.

What must be checked next

Next we need to check each source’s runtime and licensing, component availability, prototype behaviour, field safety, facilitators and the support we can actually provide. Detailed inclusions and refund terms will be checked against the season plan. Real activity records will be labelled separately. Language support for on-site activities has not yet been confirmed.
