HACKER FARM / STUDIO · 2026-10-11 · v13

Follow the preparation

We start with public Hackerfarm Japan projects and design ways to test them at our farm. These are preparation protocols, not field results or member activity records.

01 / Reference

TechRice ↗

Our adaptation

Build a comparison sheet combining photos, handwritten observations and sensor readings from one place before buying equipment.

What we will test

Observe the same position for seven days and record gaps, errors and reasons for repeat measurements. No field data yet.

02 / Reference

QR Inventory ↗

Our adaptation

Link each tool to a name, location and condition, with an equivalent paper card for people who do not use QR codes.

What we will test

Compare retrieval time and wrong selections using paper and QR cards. This field test is planned.

03 / Reference

Solar Paper Lantern ↗

Our adaptation

Explore light through folded paper. Electrical components will start with a low-voltage option after a safety review.

What we will test

Photograph three folds under the same light. The current website graphic is an expressive sketch, not a physical experiment.

Our evidence standard

Publish the date, participant consent, original photos, method, failures and revisions together. Label unperformed work as planned. There are currently zero recruited members, and the field pilot is in preparation.
